Project research

AgriWise AI: An Intelligent Crop Lifecycle Decision Support System

A decision-support prototype combining machine learning, weather intelligence, lifecycle context, and farmer-confirmed activity history.

Problem and research gap

Agricultural decisions recur throughout cultivation, while basic digital tools often isolate crop selection, weather, and task reminders. This prototype explores joining these contexts across a crop lifecycle and preserving the difference between a recommended action and a farmer-confirmed action.

Proposed contribution

AgriWise combines crop classification, forecast observations, transparent configured rules, lifecycle profiles, explanations, and an auditable farm activity log. It is an academic decision-support prototype, not an autonomous farming system or validated agronomic diagnostic.

Closed-loop agricultural decision support

Observe conditions→Recommend action→Farmer performs or skips→Confirm status→Store activity→Use history in future rules

Pending and skipped activities are never interpreted as completed. No autonomous field action is claimed.

System components

Machine learning
Decision Tree, Random Forest, and KNN comparison on the supplied crop dataset.

Expert system
Readable IF-THEN screening rules for rain, heat, nutrients, disease environment, and confirmed recent activity.

Lifecycle context
Crop stage and stage-specific activities based on configured duration profiles.

Weather intelligence
Open-Meteo current conditions and seven-day forecast with configured risk thresholds.

All values and advice are educational decision support. Confirm important decisions with local soil tests and qualified agricultural guidance.
