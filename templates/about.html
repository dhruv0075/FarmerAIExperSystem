About AgriWise AI

AgriWise AI is an intelligent agricultural decision-support system that integrates machine learning, weather data, and expert rules to guide farmers from crop selection to harvest.

Artificial Intelligence: The system supports farmers with data-informed decisions.

Machine Learning: Crop classification is performed using a trained classifier on soil and climate features.

Expert System: IF-THEN agricultural rules evaluate crop stage, weather conditions, soil health, and nutrient status.

Inference Engine: Rules are evaluated on the current field situation to generate advice.

Explainable AI: Each recommendation includes reasons and confidence information to make decisions understandable.

This project demonstrates a unified decision-support approach, combining crop recommendation, weather analysis, lifecycle tracking, and advisory generation.
